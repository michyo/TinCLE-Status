Scope: tinclec 0.18, Windows / TinyCC.

The bundled SDL2_gfx.dll also exports SDL_ functions. tinclec links SDL2.dll before SDL2_gfx.dll, and TinyCC can resolve the game's SDL_ imports to SDL2_gfx.dll instead of SDL2.dll. This can cause entry-point failures if an otherwise compatible SDL2_gfx runtime is used and can mix SDL implementations.

Suggested fix: ensure SDL2.dll is passed after SDL2_gfx.dll, or restrict SDL2_gfx.dll exports to the gfx API. Add an import-table check to the compiler assistant.
